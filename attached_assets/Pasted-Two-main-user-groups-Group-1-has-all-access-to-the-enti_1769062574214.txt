Two main user groups
- Group 1 has all access to the entire platform, administering Group 2

- Group 2 is divided into 3 sub-groups. Group A administers the remaining 2 subgroups B and C.

Call Group 1 , Super Administrator. Call subgroup A, Trucker. Call subgroup B, Driver and call subgroup C, Helper.

Super Administrator creates and administers Truckers, and can see all activities of the Truckers.

Truckers Add Trucks to their platform, they also add Drivers and Helpers to their platform.

The Trucker should be able to be reminded (via phone, text message and email) about any Truck license about to expire in 1 month. Reminders should go out 1 month to Expiry, 2 weeks to Expiry and 3 days to Expiry only when reminders have not been turned off by either the Super Administrator or the Trucker.

The Trucker should be able to add Drivers and Helpers to his platform. Trucker should be able to assign and unassign a Driver and a Helper to a truck.

A trucker should be able to see how many days a Driver or Helper has worked so as to be able to pay them appropriately bi-weekly.

A Driver and Helper should be able to see how many days they've worked and should be able to see how much they would be paid and their next pay due date.

Drivers and Helpers will be able to check in and check out to work, daily, this is important to calculate their bi-weekly pay.

Drivers and Helpers would also be reminded (via text message, phone call and email) about their expiring license.
